The problem

Student records drift out of sync

Student-level academic information can contain missing identifiers, incomplete records, spelling variations, inconsistent details and potential duplicate enrolments. Manual checking and correction make it hard to find problems, track requests and keep records reliable.

  • Missing or malformed APAAR identifiers
  • Name and date-of-birth inconsistencies
  • Possible duplicate or cross-institution enrolments
  • Slow, untracked correction and grievance handling

The proposed solution

One platform from upload to verification

AcadID is a centralised workspace that helps authorised institutions manage records linked to APAAR IDs, validate submitted data, identify inconsistencies, guide correction requests, track grievances and support privacy-conscious verification.

Upload→Validate→Detect→Correct→Review→Verify→Analyze

Features

Everything needed to keep records trustworthy

Bulk CSV import

Preview, validate and import records with row-level errors, duplicate detection and a downloadable error report.

Smart mismatch detection

Normalised and fuzzy matching with configurable thresholds flags issues with severity and recommended actions.

Pre-flight checker

Compare document details with a record and get a personalised checklist in English and Tamil.

Correction tracking

Ticket IDs, status history, information requests, internal notes and overdue indicators.

Consent-based proof links

Time-limited, revocable tokens that disclose only the claims a student chooses to share.

Analytics dashboards

Intake versus enrolment, data quality and exceptions, all computed from the records and consistent with exports.

Audit trail

Append-only log of imports, edits, approvals, exports and verification activity.

Reports and exports

CSV downloads and print-to-PDF views that respect your role and filters.

Workflow

Detect → Fix → Verify

1

Detect

Upload data, run validation and see missing fields, invalid identifiers, possible duplicates and name or date-of-birth mismatches.

2

Fix

Students run a pre-flight check, raise a correction request and respond to questions. Administrators review, approve or reject with reasons.

3

Verify

With consent, a student generates a time-limited link or QR code that shows a verifier only the permitted claims.

Data privacy and security

Designed around data minimisation

  • Role-based views: students see only their own record; institution administrators see only their institution
  • APAAR IDs are masked in lists and never placed in links or QR codes
  • Proof links use random tokens with expiry, revocation and access logs
  • Date of birth, marks and identity numbers are never shared by default
  • Fuzzy matches never change an official record automatically
  • Audit entries avoid passwords, tokens and unnecessary personal data

About this prototype

This version runs entirely in your browser and stores synthetic demo data in local browser storage. It has no server, so role checks run in the page itself. A production deployment needs a hardened backend, a real database and authorised integrations.

No real student information or government identifiers are used. Email, SMS, OCR and official verification are not connected and are labelled as simulated or unavailable wherever they appear.

Help

Frequently asked questions

Is AcadID an official AICTE, APAAR or DigiLocker service?

No. AcadID is an independent prototype. The AICTE logo marks the use case being addressed and does not imply endorsement or partnership.

Is the data real?

No. All institutions, students and identifiers are fictional and generated for the demonstration.

Does a high similarity score mean a duplicate or fraud?

No. A score highlights something worth checking. Authorised reviewers decide, and records are never changed or merged automatically.

What does a proof link reveal?

Only the claims the student selected, such as institution, programme or enrolment status, until the link expires or is revoked. It never contains personal data or an APAAR ID.

Can I try every role?

Yes. Open Sign in and use the clearly labelled demo accounts for student, institution administrator, AICTE administrator (demo role) and academic verifier.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser's local storage on this device. Settings has a button to reset the demo data.
